# Saathi product spec: autonomy settings, the fence, the wording, the goals and the manual pilot

## 1. Goal, scope and marks

Goal: how much the caregiver sees and how often a tap is needed are settings of a household, the experimental phone agent cannot reach a second household, and the emergency promise is stated the same way everywhere. This file holds RQ-112 to RQ-116 (section 2), the goals G-1 to G-12 (section 3) and RQ-117 to RQ-119 (section 4). Marks: PRD section 2. All rows are P unless the state column says otherwise. The phase of each row is in the PRD phase table.

## 3. Goals

Marks are defined in PRD-saathi-2026-11-09-r2.md section 2. Each target is P. A rate claim carries its rule-of-three bound (zero events in n trials bound the rate at 3/n, 95%). Calls between the elder and the caregiver are of two kinds, set in the `/called` reason class (assurance RQ-68): task calls (a ride, an order, a question the helper can prepare) and social calls (contact for its own sake). Social calls are not a target to reduce; a fall in them is a harm signal (D-86).

| ID | Goal | Target | Method and sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-1 | Task calls avoided | In-scope task calls fall 80% against a baseline period. Social calls are not a target to reduce (D-86). | `/called` rows with reason class task or social (assurance RQ-68), 2 weeks before launch and after Phase 2; the manual pilot gives the first baseline (pilot RQ-117). A percentage needs 20 or more baseline task calls. |
| G-2 | Caregiver time per approval | Median 30 s or less from card delivery to decision, for cards answered within 5 min | `card_sent` and `decided` audit timestamps, 30 or more cards, median and p90.  Delivery is the proxy for opening (N). |
| G-3 | Request to card | p90 under 3 min for food and groceries, under 4 min for rides | Run record timestamps (assurance RQ-55), 30 or more requests per class, shadow runs included. |
| G-4 | First-pass approval | 85% or more of cards approved with no edit or reject | Wilson 95% interval. A lower bound above 85% needs at least 57 of 60. |
| G-5 | Wrong commits | 0, as a design invariant | Policy tests pass; observed wrong live commits are reported with 3/n. |
| G-6 | Emergency and scam recall | Every positive in the radar set is flagged | 100 or more positives per class in Hindi, Hinglish and English is the target. The Phase 0 set is about 100 phrases of emergency, scam and normal messages in Hindi and Hinglish, written by the caregiver (safety RQ-16, D-95); below the target the report states n and the n still needed. Zero misses in 100 bound recall at 97%; false alarms per 100 benign messages are reported beside it. |
| G-7 | Debunk quality | 85% verdict accuracy and 0 fabricated sources on at least 60 held-out claims | Zero fabrications in 60 bound the rate at 5%. |
| G-8 | Abstention | Reported as its own class, no target | Router and debunk abstentions per week. |
| G-9 | Paid model spend | At or under the cap in private config | `llm_spend` ledger, monthly (assurance RQ-48). |
| G-10 | First response latency | p95 of elder messages get an acknowledgement or a reply within 10 s | `inbox.received_at` to the first outbound message, at least 100 messages (requests RQ-74). |
| G-11 | Cart and ride correctness | n = 30 blind-labelled quotes per class; threshold 0 mismatches (zero in 30 bound the rate at 10%) | Shadow quotes (SPEC evidence section 3). ## 4. Manual pilot

Goal: learn, with no code and no AI, whether the elder uses a helper channel at all, which requests the elder makes and how long a person takes, before the Phase 1 scope is fixed. Scope: one household, 2 to 4 weeks, in parallel with Phase 0. This file holds RQ-117 to RQ-119. The pilot and the Phase 0 build do not wait for each other, with two exceptions: the elder is not moved to the AI path before the terms receipt (POLICY-data PL-32) and the consent record (POLICY-data PL-33) exist, and Phase 1 scope waits for the RQ-119 receipt. Marks: PRD section 2. All rows are P unless the state column says otherwise. The phase of each row is in the PRD phase table.

The term "manual pilot" here means this activity. The pilot consent is the first consent record (POLICY-data PL-05); uses the pilot does not need are granted later by new events.

| ID | Requirement | Check | Evidence and state |
|---|---|---|---|
| RQ-117 | Protocol and log. For 2 to 4 weeks the caregiver answers the elder's requests by hand on the helper channel, with no AI and no automation, and the pilot sends no elder data to any Saathi service. The elder is told at the start, in the elder's language, that a person answers. Each request gets one log row kept by the caregiver, with coded fields only: `week` (a week number, not a date), `request_type` (an intent of D-09 or `other`), `task_or_social` (`task` or `social`), `elder_initiated` (yes or no), `minutes_to_answer` (elder message to the caregiver's first reply), `clarifications` (a count), `call_followed` (yes or no). The row holds no content, name or address. Phone calls between the two are tallied the same way (`task` or `social`) for 2 weeks before the pilot and during it, which gives the G-1 baseline (PRD Q7, Q8). | a count script rejects a row with a value outside the coded set and is shown to fail on a row with free text; the owner reads the header and 5 rows and finds no content | N |
| RQ-118 | Consent and data handling. Before the pilot, consent is spoken in the elder's language with teach-back ("tell me what this helper will do with your voice") and is recorded as the records of POLICY-data PL-33, with the notice version; where capacity is in doubt a guardian consents first (PL-34). The elder is told that a named person can be phoned, that stopping needs no reason and no explanation, and that Saathi is not an emergency service and 112 and 108 are the numbers to call (product RQ-116). An emergency or scam message goes to the family at once. No test or drill induces fear in the elder; emergency drills in the pilot exercise the caregiver side only. The log and the tallies stay in the git-ignored private folder of the code repository and never enter this repository, a trace store or a hosted service. Only aggregate counts are committed, as a receipt of the type in evidence RQ-100: counts by request type, the share elder-initiated, the task and social split, median and p90 minutes to answer, n and the week range; no date finer than a week and no content. The raw log is deleted 90 days after the pilot ends (P). | the consent records exist before the first pilot message (manual receipt); a leak scan over `git ls-files` finds no pilot file; the receipt validator accepts the aggregate receipt and rejects one with a content field | N |
| RQ-119 | Exit criteria and Phase 1 scope. The pilot ends at 4 weeks, or at 2 weeks when checks 1 to 3 below are already met. Thresholds are P and are written, with the plan, before the pilot starts. The exit receipt gives counts with n for: (1) habit: the elder initiated at least 1 in-scope request in each of the last 2 weeks; (2) request mix: an intent with at least 3 requests stays in the Phase 1 build list, an intent with fewer is deferred and recorded as not applicable with a re-entry condition, and the list narrows D-09 only through a new D number; (3) minutes to answer by hand, the baseline for G-2 and G-3; (4) the task and social call split against the baseline, for G-1 and G-12; (5) the elder's own answer, asked once and spoken, on whether they would rather phone. The outcome is `go` (1 to 3 met), `narrow` (1 met and the list reduced) or `stop` (1 not met after 4 weeks, or the elder prefers to phone, or social calls fell as task calls fell). `stop` pauses the build beyond Phase 0 until the owner decides. The Phase 0 gate row is verified when the receipt exists with an outcome; `go` or `narrow` fixes the Phase 1 build list. One household gives counts and no rates: no figure here is evidence about other households. | receipt validator: outcome present, n and week range present, no content field; a test that the Phase 1 build list is derived from the receipt's intent counts; the receipt is shown to be refused when the outcome is missing | N |

## 5. Out of scope and order of work

Out of scope: elder self-approval under a cap and a digest-only mode (approval modes beyond the two above), a caregiver web app, any change to the radar, goals for households other than the first, any money target (there is none; POLICY PL-36), any recording or transcript of the elder's messages in the pilot, a second pilot household, and paying or rewarding participants (a family matter). Order of work: the PRD phase table. The settings and the fence come in Phase 1 with the cards and adapters; the wording comes in Phase 0 with the fixed clips. For the pilot: record consent (RQ-118), tally the two baseline weeks, start, log each request (RQ-117), write the exit receipt (RQ-119); the Phase 0 build runs beside it.

Skipped or not checked: figures; no setting, manifest or sign-off row exists in code; no pilot has run and no target was measured; the Hindi wording of the sentence was not reviewed by a Hindi reader; the 20% and 8 week values of RQ-114 and G-12 and the pilot thresholds are proposals; the comfort question was not written; an independent ethics read was not arranged, which matters from the second household on.
